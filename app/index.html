About

Prototype. Recordings are sent to AssemblyAI for transcription and Claude (via AssemblyAI) for summaries, then the results are saved on this phone only.
How it works
  1. Tap the red button to record, tap it again to pause, and Stop & save at the end.
  2. In a few minutes the meeting shows under Meetings with a title, summary, action items and who spoke. Keep the app open until it says “Processing” (the work continues even if you close it, and finishes next time you open the app).
  3. Open a meeting to name the voices or add details; the AI uses them to fix the rest.
  4. Use Ask to ask questions across all your meetings.

Tips

  • Phone in the middle of the table, screen on.
  • Saying names out loud (“Thanks, Glenn”) helps the AI name people.
  • Back up your notes now and then in Settings.

Be fair and legal

Always tell people they are being recorded. Some states require everyone's permission. Check your employer's rules before recording work meetings.
