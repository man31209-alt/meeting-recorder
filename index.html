👋 Welcome

This connects the recorder to your own private notes on GitHub. It takes a minute, once. Your recordings never go anywhere else.
How do I get a phone key?
  1. On this phone, open github.com and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens, then Generate new token.
  3. Name it meeting phone. Set expiration to 1 year.
  4. Repository access: Only select repositories, then pick your notes repository.
  5. Permissions: Contents → Read and write. Nothing else.
  6. Tap Generate token, copy it, paste it in box 3.
The key stays on this phone. Never send it to anyone.

Add to home screen

iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android: Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
How it works
  1. Tap the red button to record. Tap Stop & save when the meeting ends. It uploads by itself.
  2. In a few minutes the meeting appears under Meetings, with a title, topic, summary, action items and who spoke, all filled in for you.
  3. Open it to name the voices or add details. The AI uses what you add to fix the rest.

Tips for good results

  • Phone in the middle of the table, screen on, not in a pocket.
  • Saying names out loud ("Thanks, Glenn") helps the AI name people.
  • If it shows too many or too few people, open the meeting → Wrong number of people?
  • No signal? Recordings that fail to upload are kept on this phone until you send them.

Be fair and legal

Always tell people they're being recorded. Some states require everyone's permission. Check your company's rules before recording work meetings: the audio is sent to a transcription service.
